## 👋 About us

[Cyllene](https://www.groupe-cyllene.com) is a French digital transformation company with **40+ years of experience** and **400+ experts** across cloud, infrastructure, data & AI, cybersecurity and application development.

Our e-commerce team designs and builds B2B and B2C platforms on **Sylius** and **Symfony**, and is a reference Sylius partner in France. Our projects can be audited directly by the Sylius core team, and we care about the same things in open source as in client work: clean code, solid tests and good documentation.

This organization is where we share the plugins and bundles we extract from real projects.
